Virtual Audio Interface installs a 128-channel virtual Core Audio device plus a visualizer app that shows per-channel levels and your speaker layout in 3D.

This installer restarts coreaudiod to load the driver, which briefly interrupts all audio devices on this Mac (a second or two). Close anything sensitive to audio dropouts before continuing.

After installing, open Virtual Audio Interface from Applications. In your DAW (e.g. Ableton Live), select “Virtual Audio Interface” as the audio output device to route up to 128 channels into the visualizer.
